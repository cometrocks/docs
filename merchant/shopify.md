# Connect Shopify

1. In the [Shopify App Store](https://apps.shopify.com/comet-micro-store-builder), open **Comet: Campaign Funnel Builder** and select **Install**.
2. Choose your Shopify store and review the requested access in Shopify. Approve the installation with an account that can install apps.
3. Open Comet from your Shopify admin. Confirm that the connected store is selected and that its products are available before building a Checkout Store.

For publisher integrations, ask Comet which brand organization and operations your [publisher app](/resources/apps) can access. Store installation and publisher app access are managed separately.

## Existing custom-app connections

Shopify manages new custom apps in its Dev Dashboard. Legacy custom apps already connected to Comet can still be managed from Shopify admin. If your existing connection needs new credentials or you need a custom integration, [contact Comet Support](/support) for the connection steps that apply to your store. See [Shopify's app setup guide](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/apps/install-setup-apps) for the credential flows.
